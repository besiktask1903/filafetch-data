FilaFetch privacy

Updated October 16, 2026.

FilaFetch compares public filament prices and does not require an account. The app includes Google AdMob banner, sponsored native, and optional rewarded ads. Development builds currently use Google's dedicated test ad units. Push notifications are not enabled.

Information stored on your device

Your sort and refill preferences and the expiry time of an earned ad-free period are stored locally. The catalog is not saved for offline use; an internet connection and a successful price download are required to view listings. FilaFetch does not upload your preferences or search terms. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to your operating system's backup and restoration behavior.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google's Mobile Ads SDK connects to Google to load ads and may process connection and device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. These requests are separate from the public price feed. FilaFetch does not send your filament searches, filter selections, or purchase details to the ad SDK. Google describes its processing in its advertising policy.

For live ad configurations, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain required privacy choices before requesting ads. When applicable, an Ad privacy choices control appears in Filters. The app does not request Apple's cross-app tracking permission. Sponsored cards are labeled separately from product listings. Completing an optional rewarded video pauses banners and sponsored cards for 30 minutes; watching ads is not required to search, filter, or open stores.

Network connections

The app downloads public price data from GitHub Pages and product photos from retailer image services, including Amazon, Bambu Lab, and Shopify/ELEGOO. Those providers receive the ordinary connection information needed to serve the request, such as your IP address and network headers, under their own privacy policies. These requests do not include an account, a FilaFetch user profile, or your search/filter selections.

Opening a purchase link takes you to the retailer. Purchases, sign-ins, payment details, cookies, and tracking on that retailer's website or app are handled by that retailer. FilaFetch does not receive your payment or order information.

Support

If you choose to submit a support request through GitHub, GitHub processes the information you provide under its own policies. Public issue reports are visible to others; do not include passwords or private payment details.
