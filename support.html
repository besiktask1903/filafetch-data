FilaFetch support

Print more. Spend less.

FilaFetch compares public US filament listings from SUNLU, ELEGOO and KINGROON on Amazon, Bambu Lab, and ELEGOO. Search by filament or color, choose one or more materials, and compare the price per spool or the full purchase price.

Contact

Email iamdebugging@gmail.com for help or feedback. Include your app version, device model, and a description of the issue. For a price or product-link issue, include the retailer and product name. Do not send passwords, payment details, or order documents.

You can also report an issue on GitHub. GitHub reports are public.

How current are the prices?

Checks are scheduled for midnight and noon Eastern Time. The app shows the latest published results and the completed scan's time. If a listing cannot be checked, its previous observation may remain until a later successful check. Open its details to see the individual price-check timestamp.

Why is the checkout price different?

Filament pictures are original illustrations, not product photographs. Colors are approximate, and an unknown color is shown neutrally. Confirm the exact color, finish, and product variant on the retailer's page.

Prices and availability can change after a check. Shipping, tax, coupons, memberships, and account-specific discounts are excluded. Bulk prices require buying the full quantity shown. A refill does not include a reusable spool. Confirm the selected variant, quantity, packaging, and final price with the retailer.

Why does the app need internet access?

FilaFetch downloads the current public catalog when opened and refreshes it while active. There is no offline catalog. Check your connection and tap Try again if the catalog cannot load.

Purchases and returns

Products are purchased directly from the retailer. Contact the retailer about orders, shipping, returns, or payments. FilaFetch does not process purchases or receive your order information.
