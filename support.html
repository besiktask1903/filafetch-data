FilaFetch support

Print more. Spend less.

FilaFetch compares selected filament listings from Amazon US, Bambu Lab US, and ELEGOO US. Amazon coverage includes SUNLU, ELEGOO, KINGROON, and AMOLEN. Search by filament or color, choose one or more materials, and compare the price per 1 kg spool or the full purchase price.

Contact

Email iamdebugging@gmail.com for help or feedback. Include your app version, device model, and a description of the issue. For a price or product-link issue, include the retailer and product name. Do not send passwords, payment details, or order documents.

You can also report an issue on GitHub. GitHub reports are public.

How current are the prices?

Checks are scheduled for midnight and noon Eastern Time. The app shows the latest published results and the completed scan's time. If a listing cannot be checked, its previous observation may remain until a later successful check. Tap a result to open the retailer and confirm the current price and availability.

Why is the checkout price different?

The small color dots are approximate swatches. Colors without a known swatch do not receive a guessed color. Confirm the exact color, finish, and product variant on the retailer's page.

Prices and availability can change after a check. Shipping, tax, coupons, memberships, and account-specific discounts are excluded. Bulk prices require buying the full quantity shown. A refill does not include a reusable spool. Confirm the selected variant, quantity, packaging, and final price with the retailer.

How do the price sorts work?

Per-spool sorting compares confirmed 1 kg spool quantities. Total-price sorting compares the full displayed purchase price, including multipacks. The main price follows the selected sort. A second price is shown when it differs, so you can compare both the unit cost and the purchase total. Unconfirmed or mixed-size packs show their total price without a per-1 kg-spool comparison.

Why does the app need internet access?

FilaFetch downloads the current public catalog when opened and refreshes it while active. There is no offline catalog. If loading takes longer than expected, check your connection; the app retries automatically.

iPhone and iPad Home Screen widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, search for FilaFetch, and add Today's Cheapest Spool in the small or medium size. It shows the lowest verified, available price per confirmed 1 kg spool in the latest catalog, with the full pack cost and quantity. Refills are labeled. Tap the widget to open FilaFetch.

iOS controls background widget updates. The widget requests periodic refreshes, shows the last-check time, and expires displayed offers. If a current offer cannot be loaded, open the app for the latest listings. Prices and availability can change; confirm them at the retailer.

Purchases and returns

Products are purchased directly from the retailer. Contact the retailer about orders, shipping, returns, or payments. FilaFetch does not process purchases or receive your order information.
