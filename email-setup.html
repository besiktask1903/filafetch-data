FilaFetch email setup

Do these steps on your phone. This sets up scanner reports to iamdebugging@gmail.com.

  1. Open Google App passwords. Sign in to your Gmail account and complete any verification. Google may require 2-Step Verification first.
  2. Create an app password named FilaFetch Scanner. Copy Google's 16-character app password.
  3. Return here, paste it below, and tap Create encrypted setup code.

Enter the app password, never your normal Google password. Encryption happens on your phone. This page does not send the password to a server or store it. Only your Mac has the matching private key.

Paste this encrypted code into our chat. Keep the actual app password out of the chat.
